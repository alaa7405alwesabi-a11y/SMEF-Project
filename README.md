# SME Development & Financing Platform (SMEF)

A Graduation Project Developed for the **General Authority for Small and Micro Enterprise Development and Financing

## Overview

The SME Development & Financing Platform (SMEF) is a centralized digital platform designed to support the General Authority for Small and Micro Enterprise Development and Financing by bringing information, services, and resources for small and micro enterprises into one accessible system.

The platform aims to improve access to enterprise-related services, promote small businesses, facilitate communication between users and the Authority, and provide a centralized environment for managing institutional content and service requests.

This repository serves as a **portfolio showcase** of the graduation project, highlighting selected user-interface screenshots, system analysis, database design, and project documentation.

## Project Objectives

- Centralize information and services related to small and micro enterprises.
- Improve access to information about the Authority and its initiatives.
- Support enterprise visibility and promotion.
- Facilitate communication between users and the Authority.
- Digitize selected service-request and enterprise-submission workflows.
- Provide administrative tools for managing website content and requests.

## Key Features

### Public Portal

- About the Authority: Information about the Authority, its mission, and activities.
- Services: Access to available services and related request forms.
- Government Facilities: Information about facilities and support available to enterprises.
- Authority Projects: A dedicated section for presenting institutional projects.
- Enterprise Promotion: A submission workflow that allows enterprise owners to request publication of their projects, subject to administrative approval.
- Media and Awareness: News, announcements, media content, and awareness materials.
- Success Stories: A section highlighting enterprise achievements and success stories.
- Interactive Map: A geographical view of relevant projects and locations.
- Contact and Communication: Contact forms and communication channels.
- AI-Powered Chatbot: An assistant designed to help visitors find information and navigate available resources.

### User and Service Management

- User registration and authentication.
- Service-request submission and management.
- Enterprise registration and promotion forms.
- Request tracking and relevant historical records.
- Role-based access for customers, employees, and administrators.

### Administrative Dashboard

- Centralized content and website management.
- Management of pages, forms, and media.
- Management of submitted requests and enterprise listings.
- Administrative review and approval of submitted projects.
- Role-based permissions for different administrative responsibilities.
